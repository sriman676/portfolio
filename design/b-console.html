    Hybrid Identity Detection and Risk Architecture, sole author

    Under review

    Models the attack surface as a Temporal Attack-Surface Graph with eight node types and treats non-human identities as first-class security objects.

  • FedDecept

    First author, with Lakshmi Rishitha Dintakurthi and Naga Sravya Nanduru; supervised by Dr. Satish Anamalamudi

    Under review

    Lets organisations coordinate honeypot and deception strategy through federated learning without sharing raw data, with Byzantine-robust aggregation that holds under poisoning.

    • d = 2.86Cohen's effect size, 8 seeds
    • ≈ 48%improvement vs isolated operation
    • ≈ 79%of centralized oracle gain recovered

  • AXON

    Post-quantum QUIC transport

    Under review

    A post-quantum QUIC transport that moves provenance and capability enforcement from application metadata into the wire, with capability attenuation verified in SMT and channel binding, non-forgeability and revocation ordering verified in Tamarin. Under review at a journal.

    • 1.55%transport overhead, vs 13× for per-frame signing (130 tests)
    • 0 unsafeRust implementation
