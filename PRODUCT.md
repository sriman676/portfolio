# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
1. **Recruiters and hiring managers** who skim: they need name, role focus, proof (papers, internship, projects), availability and a contact in seconds, with or without the 3D.
2. **Engineers and researchers** who dig into case studies, papers and code.
3. **Search engines and AI agents** reading HTML, JSON-LD, `/llms.txt` and `/agents`.

## Product Purpose
The portfolio of Sriman Rutvik Voddiraju, a cybersecurity researcher and builder (SOC operations, detection
engineering, applied AI for security). It must make a recruiter want to reach out, and make an engineer believe the
work is real. Success: a recruiter reaches the résumé or email in ≤ 2 clicks; an engineer spends time in a case study.

## Brand Personality
Precise, curious, calm in the facts; bold, cinematic, playful in the 3D moments; serious and technical in the
content. The concept is "signal in deep space": a physically accurate black hole and galaxy (real relativity and
optics, not decoration) that you can explore Bruno-Simon-style, framing an engineer's plain, verifiable record.
Wonder in the world, no hype in the words.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI portfolio tells (SPEC §3): all-caps eyebrow labels, identical card grids with the same shadow, a single
  accented word in headlines, 01/02/03 markers on things that aren't sequences, gradient text, glass cards.
- Neon "hacker" clichés: green-on-black matrix rain as the main look, skull/hoodie imagery, fake terminal spam.
- Stock-3D space sites: textured Earth spinning, lens-flare overload, sci-fi HUD noise that hurts legibility.
- SaaS landing structure: hero metric + logo wall + three feature cards + pricing-style CTA.
- References that fit: bruno-simon.com (the world IS the page, exploration as play); the *Interstellar* black hole
  (accuracy as beauty).

## Design Principles
1. **Facts first, world second.** Every claim is readable HTML; the 3D frames it and never hides it.
2. **Accuracy is the aesthetic.** Physics, colour and motion come from real equations; no fake glow to fake drama.
3. **One wow per moment.** Each scroll beat earns one strong effect; everything around it stays quiet.
4. **Play, never a trap.** Exploration is optional and reversible; recruiters can always read the page directly.
5. **Show the work, not adjectives.** Status, metrics and roles are exact and verifiable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard-complete, visible focus, focus trapped in dialogs. `prefers-reduced-motion` gets the still
image and the plain page (no flight, no flash). No-WebGL and low-power devices get the full HTML and a ray-traced
still. Text over 3D must keep ≥ 4.5:1 contrast. No sound effects. The horizon flash is a single soft flash (never
repeated), within WCAG 2.3.1.
